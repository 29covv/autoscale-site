Privacy Policy

AutoScale Systems · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how personal information is handled when you visit the AutoScale Systems website, contact us or book an AI automation demonstration. It covers our website and sales enquiries; any future client implementation will be subject to its own appropriate data protection documentation and agreements.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

AutoScale Systems is the trading name used for this business. The business is responsible for deciding how personal data submitted for website enquiries and demo bookings is used.

Contact email: hello@autoscalesystems.io
Correspondence address: Office 11, The Renewal Trust Business Centre, 3 Hawksworth Street, Nottingham, NG3 2EG, United Kingdom.

Written privacy enquiries may be sent by email or to the correspondence address above.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, business email, telephone number and brokerage name.
  • Information about the challenges you wish to discuss and any messages you send us.
  • Demo booking details, including selected dates and times, booking status and answers to booking questions.
  • Your marketing preference, if you choose to provide it, including the date and source of the choice where available.
  • Limited technical data that may be generated by our hosting provider or embedded third-party services, as described in their notices.

Please do not send us confidential borrower information, financial records or sensitive personal information through the general enquiry or scheduling forms.

3. Why we use this information

  • To manage enquiries and demos: to respond to your request, arrange a meeting, send joining instructions and follow up about the requested service. Our lawful basis will generally be legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries or steps requested before a contract, as applicable.
  • To keep business records: to manage our relationship, protect our systems and handle disputes where necessary, relying on legitimate interests or legal obligations as appropriate.
  • For optional marketing: where you actively opt in, to send occasional promotional messages about AutoScale services and AI automation. Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

We will not treat booking a demo, leaving a marketing question unanswered or selecting a refusal as consent to promotional email marketing.

4. Scheduling and service providers

Our booking calendar is provided by Calendly. When you use the embedded calendar, information you enter is processed through Calendly to arrange and manage your booking. Calendly may use its own cookies and similar technologies within the embedded scheduler; its own notices and controls apply.

Google Calendar is connected to check availability and store appointments. Online meetings may be held through Microsoft Teams. The website is hosted through Vercel. If lead information is later connected to Zapier, Google Sheets or an email marketing system, this policy will be updated before those uses go live.

5. Sharing and international transfers

Personal data may be shared with service providers to the extent necessary for the purposes described above. Some services may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data protection law requires an international transfer safeguard, we will assess the applicable mechanism and provider terms.

We do not sell personal information.

6. How long information is kept

Enquiry and booking records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential business relationship and meet relevant legal obligations. Marketing preferences are retained while relevant to demonstrating and respecting your choice. Unnecessary records will be periodically reviewed and deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you may request access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and object to certain processing. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time by contacting hello@autoscalesystems.io or using any unsubscribe link included in a marketing email.

You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

8. Security

We aim to use appropriate access controls, account security and reputable service providers, taking account of the information involved. No internet transmission or service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

This notice may be updated as the business and its website services develop. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
