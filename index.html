Data Protection

Your clients' data. Your control.

Your clients trust you with sensitive information. We take that responsibility seriously. Every automation starts with a review of your existing systems, the information involved and who should have access. Nothing goes live until the approach has been agreed with you.

01

Your existing systems

We work with the platforms you already use wherever technically feasible. There's no requirement to move your business onto our own platform.

02

Only the data that's needed

We design each workflow to use the minimum information necessary. For document-chasing workflows, the aim is to track what's been received, not analyse the contents.

03

Access you approve

We agree what each automation can access and what it can do. Permissions are assessed before implementation, with sensitive decisions remaining under your control.

04

Agreed in writing

Before anything goes live, we document the proposed data flow, storage, access, retention and relevant responsibilities so you know how the workflow will operate.

Designed around UK GDPR

We consider data protection from the beginning, including whether processing agreements, risk assessments and additional safeguards are required for your particular workflow.

Questions brokers ask

Straight answers.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We set it up, test it and support it. Your team keeps using the tools it already uses.

Can I start with just one service?

Yes, and we recommend it. Every client starts with a single workflow on a 30-day pilot, then we add more only if it's earning its place.

What if we already use a CRM or case management system?

Good. We build around it rather than replacing it, wherever technically feasible, so your team can keep working with familiar tools.

Will implementing automation disrupt our day-to-day business?

No major changes to the way your team works should be necessary. We start with one workflow, build around your existing systems and test everything before it goes live. Your team can continue working as usual while we introduce automation gradually.

Will AI read my clients' bank statements or identity documents?

Not as part of our proposed initial document-chasing workflow. The aim is to track document status and send appropriate reminders. Any workflow involving document contents would require a separate assessment and your agreement.

Where will my clients' information be stored?

That depends on your existing systems and the agreed design. We review your current storage and integrations first, then explain where data will be held or transferred before implementation.

How do you protect my clients' data?

We plan data handling from the start, with UK GDPR in mind. We assess what information each workflow needs, who should have access, and where information goes. We agree the proposed safeguards with you in writing before anything goes live.

Can I review the security arrangements before committing?

Yes. We discuss the proposed data flow, permissions, responsibilities and safeguards with you before a workflow goes live. We also start by testing with sample data.

Does the AI give advice or pick lenders?

No. Advice, lender selection and final terms stay with you. You decide what needs your approval.

Will it replace my staff?

No. It takes repetitive work off your team so they can spend more time with clients and lenders.
